# Alandas System 1 Locked Scope

## Why now

Sidy already has a tea business that works offline. The problem is that growth depends too much on manual work:

- cafe orders arrive through WhatsApp photos or voice notes
- invoices depend on Dolibarr and the old Hermes flow
- leads are not captured in one clean place
- Meta access is unclear
- the EUR 19 tasting box needs a cleaner path into recurring cafe accounts

System 1 turns this into a simple revenue machine before any heavier architecture is built.

## Business target

Primary target: move toward 100 active wholesale cafe accounts.

System 1 target: produce the first clean batch of qualified cafe leads and draft Sidy-approved outreach for the EUR 19 discovery tasting box.

## Included

### 1. Lead generation and qualification

Build a first cafe lead batch from German city clusters.

Priority cities:

- Berlin
- Munich
- Hamburg

Fit rules:

- cafe, brunch restaurant, boutique hotel, or specialty coffee venue
- likely seating above 30
- visible food or drink quality
- likely fit for loose-leaf tea service
- not a low-margin kiosk or random snack shop

### 2. Lead enrichment

Use the cheapest reliable source first.

Order:

1. public website
2. German Impressum or contact page
3. Instagram profile
4. Google profile
5. paid enrichment tool only after cost approval

Paid tools are capped at EUR 60 unless Sidy approves more in writing.

### 3. Outreach drafting

Draft messages for Sidy to review.

Allowed claims:

- Alandas offers loose-leaf tea for cafes
- the discovery tasting box costs EUR 19
- the kit includes teapot, tray, spoon, and samples
- cafe tea margins can be high when priced correctly
- Sidy personally handles setup and questions

Blocked claims:

- do not claim all teas are certified organic
- do not claim guaranteed profit
- do not claim stock availability that Sidy has not confirmed
- do not send messages without Sidy's approval

### 4. Dolibarr and Hermes recovery plan

Prepare the repair path for:

- customer records
- order parsing
- invoice draft creation
- backup and restore checks
- schema validation before invoice creation

No production write happens until credentials and test access are confirmed.

### 5. Meta access discovery

Ask Sidy for screenshots of:

- Meta Business Suite
- Ad Center
- Lead Center
- connected Instagram account
- connected Facebook page

This is discovery only. No ad spend is started in System 1 without separate approval.

## Excluded

System 1 does not include:

- autonomous customer messaging
- Meta ad spend
- a full LangGraph or MCP platform
- Tier 2 policy engine
- Tier 3 multi-agent system
- equity or revenue-share agreement
- Shopify production changes without collaborator access
- Dolibarr production writes without backup proof

## Done means

System 1 is done when these are true:

- first lead CSV passes validation
- Sidy has approved the outreach draft pattern
- first lead batch is ready for manual or approved dispatch
- access gaps are documented
- Dolibarr and Hermes repair path is written with next actions

This is like packing the delivery route before the driver leaves. The list must be clean before anyone knocks on doors.
