04 // Channel Strategy

B2C Has a Different Problem

B2C e-commerce and B2B wholesale have fundamentally different unit economics and must be treated as separate, parallel workstreams.

B2B Wholesale Engine

Account-Level Relationship & Retention

  • Target Buyer: Cafe owners, F&B managers, boutique hoteliers, head baristas
  • Sales Cycle: 7 to 21 days from sample trial to Starter Crate installation
  • Order Value: €249 Day-1 setup + €150–€300/month recurring refills
  • Core Bottleneck: Lead qualification, structured follow-up & reorder automation
  • Operating Goal: Build 30–50 compounding accounts generating €75k–€150k/yr
  • Strategic Value: High Customer Lifetime Value, low churn, predictable cashflow
B2C E-Commerce Funnel

Consumer Traffic, Conversion & Gifting

  • Target Buyer: Premium home tea enthusiasts, aesthetic lifestyle buyers, gift givers
  • Sales Cycle: Impulse / 24-hour decision on mobile
  • Order Value: €25 to €60 average order value (AOV)
  • Core Bottleneck: Storefront UX drop-offs, shipping threshold conflict, cart abandonment
  • Operating Goal: Fix GA4 tracking, optimize checkout, automate retention emails
  • Strategic Value: Brand awareness, consumer prestige, Q4 holiday gifting spikes
